# Al-Andalus Center
### Computer & CCTV Services

Established in **2006**, Al-Andalus Center is a technology and IT services provider based in El-Shohada, Menoufia, Egypt. With over 20 years of experience in computer hardware, technical maintenance, networking, and CCTV technologies, we provide reliable products and integrated solutions designed to meet the evolving needs of individuals, businesses, and organizations.

We specialize in sourcing and supplying new, used, and imported computers and laptops, offering a range of devices to suit different requirements and budgets. Beyond hardware sales, our expertise extends to professional computer maintenance, system upgrades, troubleshooting, and technical support, ensuring that our customers get the most out of their technology.

Our work also covers networking infrastructure, monitoring and surveillance systems, intelligent alert solutions, and specialized software development. We focus on building practical, dependable systems that improve connectivity, strengthen security, simplify daily operations, and provide better control over technical environments.

By combining quality hardware, hands-on technical expertise, and integrated software solutions, Al-Andalus Center aims to be more than a place to purchase computers — we strive to be a long-term technology partner our customers can depend on.

## Our Services & Specialties

- **Computers & Laptops:** Supplying new, used, and imported PCs and laptops, with solutions for different performance requirements and budgets.
- **Computer Maintenance & Technical Support:** Hardware diagnostics, component replacement, repairs, system optimization, upgrades, software troubleshooting, and ongoing technical assistance.
- **Networking Solutions:** Network planning, installation, configuration, infrastructure setup, connectivity troubleshooting, and network maintenance.
- **Monitoring & Surveillance Systems:** Installation and configuration of monitoring systems, surveillance equipment, and centralized monitoring solutions.
- **Security & Alert Systems:** Intelligent alert and notification systems designed to detect events, monitor environments, and provide timely notifications.
- **Software & Integrated Solutions:** Developing and implementing practical software solutions for monitoring, automation, system management, and operational control.

## Our Philosophy

We believe that technology should be accessible, dependable, and built around real-world needs. A good technology solution is not simply about having the latest hardware or software; it is about choosing the right tools, implementing them correctly, and ensuring they continue to perform reliably.

Our approach combines practical experience, technical precision, and a commitment to customer satisfaction. Whether providing an individual computer, maintaining an existing system, establishing a network, or implementing a complete monitoring solution, we focus on delivering results that customers can trust.
